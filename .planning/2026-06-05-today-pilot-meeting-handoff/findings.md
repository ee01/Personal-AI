## External References

- Zoom AI Companion supports in-meeting preset questions such as catch-up, name mentions, action items, dates, and decisions, with visible host start/stop controls.
- Microsoft Teams Facilitator keeps agenda timing visible, stores editable AI notes in Loop, and requires explicit acceptance before syncing follow-up tasks to Planner.
- RingCentral AI Assistant documentation emphasizes user activation, transcripts/notes/action items, visual indicators, and non-training commitments.
- Microsoft Research CHI 2025 work on meeting goal reflection found passive cues lower disruption while active interventions can trigger immediate action but risk interrupting the meeting.
- Prospective reflection research argues meeting assistants should help clarify purpose, success conditions, and expected challenges before the meeting.
