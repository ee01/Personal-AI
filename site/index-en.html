How It Works

Personal AI is not just a dashboard. It is a memory loop that follows your daily work surfaces.

01

Capture

Important sources become structured memories with source, reason, evidence, and future triggers.

02

Consolidate

Memory Service scores salience, links entities, applies decay, and keeps raw evidence available for review.

03

Recall

Context-aware retrieval brings back relevant memories only when the current page, message, issue, or meeting provides enough signal.

04

Assist

Suggestions, briefs, and context packs stay previewable and reversible before anything is inserted, copied, or sent elsewhere.

Designed For Trust

The system can be autonomous internally without turning the user into a daily review queue.

Evidence first

Relevant memories show why they matched and where the source came from.

Low interruption

Weak matches stay quiet; strong context appears as small cues, cards, or context packs.

Clear boundaries

Personal AI does not auto-send replies, silently externalize private context, or promote browsing into stable profile facts.
